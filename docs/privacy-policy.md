# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 22, 2026

hlthy ("we", "our", or "us") is a fitness and social media application operated by an individual developer based in Ontario, Canada. We are the data controller responsible for your personal information.

**Contact:**
Email: support@hlthy.ca
Website: https://hlthy.ca

---

## 2. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the hlthy mobile application (iOS and Android) and website at hlthy.ca, why we collect it, who we share it with, and your rights over it.

---

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Information you provide directly

- **Account information**: your name, username, email address, date of birth (for age verification), profile photo, and password (stored as a secure hash; we never see your plain-text password)
- **Profile information**: fitness archetype, bio, and any other details you add to your profile
- **Content you post**: workout posts, milestone records (personal records, weight goals), photos, captions, comments, and replies
- **Messages**: direct messages you send to other users
- **AI coaching conversations**: messages you send to the hlthy AI coaching feature

### 3.2 Information collected automatically

- **Usage data**: features you use, screens you visit, actions you take (likes, follows, posts), and timestamps
- **Device information**: device type, operating system and version, app version, and device identifiers (such as an installation ID)
- **Crash and performance data**: error logs and app performance metrics
- **Push notification tokens**: a device token used to deliver push notifications to you

### 3.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise GPS location. We do not access your contacts. We do not collect payment information (hlthy is currently free). We do not serve advertising and do not share data with advertising networks.

## 5. AI coaching and Google Gemini

The hlthy AI coaching feature is powered by **Google Gemini**, a third-party AI service operated by Google LLC. When you send a message to the AI coach:

- Your message text is transmitted to Google's servers for processing.
- Google processes this data in accordance with its own privacy policy and data processing terms. You can review Google's AI terms at https://ai.google.dev/terms.
- We do not send your name, email address, or account identifiers to Google alongside your message. Messages are sent with a session-scoped identifier only.
- Google may retain conversation data in accordance with its own retention policies. We encourage you to avoid sharing sensitive personal information (such as medical details or financial information) in AI coaching conversations.

By using the AI coaching feature, you acknowledge that your messages will be processed by Google Gemini.

---

## 6. International data transfers

hlthy is operated from Canada. However, some of the services we use process data outside Canada:

- **Firebase (Google LLC)**: authentication, database, storage, and analytics are hosted in Google's cloud infrastructure, including servers in the United States. Your AI coaching conversations are processed on servers in `us-central1` (Iowa, USA).
- **Sentry**: crash reports and error logs are processed by Functional Software Inc. (Sentry), based in the United States.
- **Google Gemini**: AI coaching messages are processed by Google LLC in the United States.

These transfers are made under appropriate safeguards. Firebase and Sentry are covered by Google's and Sentry's respective data processing agreements and standard contractual clauses where applicable. By using hlthy, you consent to these transfers.

---

## 7. Content moderation

hlthy is a social platform. To protect our community:

- All posts and content are subject to our Terms of Service (including the content rules in Section 4).
- Users can report content or other users at any time using the in-app report function.
- We review reports and take action (including content removal and account suspension) typically within 24 hours.
- Users can block other users at any time from within the app.

We may use automated tools to detect content that violates our policies. Final moderation decisions are made by a human reviewer.

---

## 8. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share your data only in the following circumstances:

- **With other hlthy users**: content you post publicly is visible to all users. Content set to "followers only" is visible only to your approved followers. Your username and profile are always publicly visible.
- **With service providers**: Firebase (Google), Sentry, and Google Gemini as described above. These providers are contractually bound to process data only as instructed by us.
- **For legal compliance**: if required by law, a court order, or a regulatory authority, we may disclose information about you. We will notify you where legally permitted to do so.
- **In a business transfer**: if hlthy is acquired or its assets are transferred, user data may be part of the transaction. We will notify you before this occurs and you will have the right to delete your account.

---

## 9. Data retention

| Data type | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Account and profile information | Until you delete your account |
| Posts, workouts, milestones | Until you delete them, or until your account is deleted |
| Direct messages | Until you or the other party deletes them, or until your account is deleted |
| AI coaching conversations | Until you clear them or delete your account; also subject to Google's retention policies on their side |
| Crash logs and analytics | 90 days |
| Push notification tokens | Until you revoke notification permissions or delete your account |

When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the following rights over your personal data:

**All users:**
- **Access**: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correction**: correct inaccurate information in your account at any time via Settings
- **Deletion**: delete your account and personal data via Settings > Delete Account, by emailing us at support@hlthy.ca, or online at hlthy.ca/delete-account

**If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR):**
- **Portability**: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Objection**: object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Restriction**: ask us to restrict processing while a complaint is resolved
- **Withdraw consent**: where processing is based on consent (e.g. push notifications), withdraw it at any time

**If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA):**
- **Know**: know what categories of personal information we collect and how it is used
- **Delete**: request deletion of your personal information
- **Non-discrimination**: we will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@hlthy.ca. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request.

## 11. Children's privacy

hlthy is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. An age gate is enforced at signup. If we become aware that a user under 16 has created an account, we will delete the account and associated data promptly. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us at support@hlthy.ca.

## 12. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

- Encryption in transit (TLS) for all data transmitted between the app and our servers
- Firebase Authentication for secure credential management (we never store plain-text passwords)
- Firebase Security Rules to restrict data access to authorised users only
- Role-based access controls for administrative functions

No system is completely secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the appropriate regulatory authority as required by law.
